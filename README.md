# ⚡ Coursera Speed Enhancer

A simple, lightweight browser extension that lets you watch Coursera lecture videos at up to **16x speed** and automatically goes to the next video when one ends.

Works on **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, **Microsoft Edge**, and **Mozilla Firefox**.

---

## 🎯 What Does It Do?

* 🚀 **Super-Fast Playback**: Plays Coursera videos at **16x speed** by default (customizable from 0.5x to 16x).
* ⏭️ **Auto-Advance**: Automatically clicks "Next Item" when a lecture finishes so you don't have to touch your mouse.
* 🛡️ **No Resetting**: Stops Coursera from forcing your speed back to 1x.
* 🔒 **100% Private**: No data collection, no account needed, and no tracking. Everything runs entirely in your browser.

## 📥 How to Install (Takes 1 Minute)

### Step 1: Download the Files
1. Click the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then click **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip (extract) the downloaded folder on your computer.

---

### Step 2: Add to Your Browser

#### 👉 For Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, or Opera:
1. Open your browser and go to the extensions page:
   * **Chrome**: Type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
   * **Brave**: Type `brave://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
   * **Edge**: Type `edge://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top-right corner).
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
4. Select the unzipped folder.
5. Done! 🎉

---

#### 👉 For Mozilla Firefox:
1. Open Firefox and type `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` in the address bar.
2. Click **Load Temporary Add-on...**
3. Select the `manifest.json` file inside the unzipped folder.
4. Done! 🎉

## 🕹️ How to Use

1. Go to [Coursera.org](https://www.coursera.org/) and open any course video.
2. The video will automatically play at **16x speed**.
3. You will see a small badge in the bottom-right corner showing the active speed.
4. When the video ends, it will automatically open the next lesson.

### Want to change the speed?
Click the **Coursera Speed Enhancer icon** in your browser's toolbar to:
* Pick a preset speed (**1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 16x**).
* Use the slider to pick any custom speed.
* Turn auto-advance on or off.
* Pause or resume the extension.

### IMPORTANT NOTICE
* If on next video the extension does not load the speed, you can simple drag the timeline pointer to the starting of the video and the extension will resume its functioning but this issue is rare
* Open to Further suggestions or feature addition, if there is any bug you noticed, you can initialize the pull request
